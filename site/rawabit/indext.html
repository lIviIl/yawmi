RawabitروابطSixteen words, four hidden groups of four. Some words look like they fit two groups.

How to play
  1. Select four words you think belong together and press Submit.
  2. A correct group locks in with its color: yellow is easiest, purple is trickiest.
  3. You can make four mistakes. "One away" means three of your four words belong together.
  4. Groups come from Egyptian food, places, music, cinema, everyday life, sports and word tricks like letters and reversals.

Rawabit (روابط) means "links". Every day a fresh grid is built from hundreds of categories, so no two days look alike.
