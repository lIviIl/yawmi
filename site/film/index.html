FilmفيلمA few emojis stand for a classic Egyptian film. Each wrong guess unlocks another clue.

How to play
  1. Look at the emojis and type the film title. Suggestions appear as you type.
  2. Each wrong guess unlocks a clue: genre, decade, lead actor, first letter and finally the year.
  3. You have six guesses.

Egyptian cinema is the shared memory of the Arab world. This game covers the golden age and the comedy hits of the 1990s and 2000s.
