MathalمَثَلThe words of a well known proverb are scrambled. Put them back in order.

How to play
  1. Tap the words in the order you think the proverb goes, then press Check.
  2. Words in the right place lock in. The rest return to the pile.
  3. After two checks you get a hint. You have five checks.

Mathal (مثل) means "proverb". Classical sayings sit next to Egyptian street wisdom, and the English meaning is shown after you finish.
